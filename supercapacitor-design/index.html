Methodology & Metrological Integrity

Evaluation Benchmarks
Current deployed model: in live testing, predictions respond only to total specific surface area (SSA). The other inputs are sent to the server but do not currently change the result.

Training Dataset: 558 carbon electrode entries from the literature (compiled from activated carbon, graphene, and porous framework studies).

Surrogate Model Architecture: Physics-Informed Gaussian Process Regression (PI-GPR). The GPR covariance kernel captures electrostatic double-layer capacitance scaling with specific surface area while calibrating epistemic uncertainty.
