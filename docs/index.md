## About

I'm a materials scientist who enjoys taking a material from synthesis all the way to a
working device, and understanding why it behaves the way it does. I hold an MSc and a BSc
in Chemistry from BUET and Khulna University, and spent four years at **The University of
Queensland** on superconducting materials for MRI. Today my work spans permanent magnets,
superconductors, and machine learning at UQ, alongside analytical chemistry at EnviroForce.
